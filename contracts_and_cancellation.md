# Contracts, Renewal and Cancellation Policy

This policy applies to Nile Max postpaid plans and NileTel Home Internet.

## Contract types

| Contract | Commitment | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Month-to-month | None | Full flexibility, standard price |
| One year | 12 months | 10% off the monthly fee and free home internet installation |
| Two year | 24 months | 15% off the monthly fee, free installation and eligibility for device installments |

## Cooling-off period

- New customers can cancel within **14 days** of signing a contract with no early termination fee. The installation or activation fee is refunded.

## Early termination fee

- **Month-to-month:** no fee. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle.
- **One year contract:** 50% of the monthly fees remaining until the contract end date.
- **Two year contract:** 50% of the remaining monthly fees plus any remaining device installments.
- The fee is waived if the customer moves to an address where NileTel cannot provide the service, or if a confirmed network fault was not fixed within 15 working days.

## Renewal

- Customers are notified by SMS 30 days before the contract end date.
- If the customer does not renew, the plan continues month-to-month at the standard (non-discounted) price.

## How to cancel

- Cancellation can be requested in a store (with national ID) or through the 1250 hotline after identity verification.
- Outstanding balances must be settled before cancellation is completed.
- Customers keeping their number with another operator (number porting) must first clear the balance; porting is completed within 24 hours.
- Before processing a cancellation, care agents must check whether the customer is eligible for a retention offer (see retention offers policy).
