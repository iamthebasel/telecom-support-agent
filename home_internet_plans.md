# NileTel Home Internet Plans (DSL and Fiber)

NileTel Home Internet is available over DSL (copper landline) and Fiber (fiber to the home). Prices are in EGP per month and include the router rental.

## Plan prices

| Plan | Technology | Speed (up to) | Monthly quota | Monthly fee (EGP) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Home DSL 30 | DSL | 30 Mbps | 200 GB | 300 |
| Home DSL 70 | DSL | 70 Mbps | 400 GB | 450 |
| Home Fiber 100 | Fiber | 100 Mbps | 600 GB | 650 |
| Home Fiber 300 | Fiber | 300 Mbps | Unlimited (fair use 2 TB) | 1,100 |

## Availability and installation

- DSL requires an active landline at the address.
- Fiber is currently available in New Cairo, Sheikh Zayed, the New Administrative Capital and parts of Alexandria. Customers can check an address in the My NileTel app.
- The installation fee is 300 EGP, waived on 12 and 24 month contracts. Installation is scheduled within 5 working days for DSL and 7 working days for Fiber.

## When the quota runs out

- After the monthly quota is used, speed is reduced to 1 Mbps until the next cycle.
- Customers can buy an Extra Quota of 50 GB for 90 EGP or 150 GB for 220 EGP at any time.

## Moving house

- Moving the service to a new address costs 150 EGP and is free for customers who have been with NileTel for more than 24 months.
- If Fiber is not available at the new address, the customer can switch to DSL without an early termination fee.
