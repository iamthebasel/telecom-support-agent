# Billing and Payment Methods

## Billing cycle

- Postpaid mobile and home internet bills are issued on the 1st of each month and are due by the 15th.
- The bill covers the monthly fee for the coming month plus any usage charges (out-of-bundle, roaming, add-ons) from the previous month.
- Plan changes made during the month are pro-rated on the next bill.

## Payment methods

NileTel accepts four payment methods. The labels in brackets are how they appear in the customer record:

- **Automatic bank transfer** ("Bank transfer (automatic)"): monthly direct debit from a bank account.
- **Automatic credit card** ("Credit card (automatic)"): the card is charged on the due date.
- **Online one-off payment** ("Electronic check"): the customer pays each bill manually in the app, by e-wallet or at a payment kiosk.
- **Paper invoice payment** ("Mailed check"): the customer receives a printed bill and pays in a store or by cheque.

Customers on automatic payment do not need a postpaid deposit and never miss a due date.

## Paperless billing

- E-bills are sent by email and in the app at no cost.
- Printed paper invoices cost 10 EGP per month.

## Late payment

- There is a 7 day grace period after the due date.
- After the grace period, a 25 EGP late fee is added and outgoing calls and data are suspended.
- If the bill is still unpaid 30 days after the due date, all services are suspended. Reconnection after payment costs 50 EGP and happens within 4 hours.

## Understanding the bill

- Itemised usage is available in the app under Bills > Details.
- Customers who think a charge is wrong should open a billing dispute (see complaints process). The disputed amount does not cause suspension while the dispute is open.
