# SIM Cards and eSIM

## New lines

- A new line requires the original national ID (or passport for foreign residents).
- A maximum of 5 NileTel mobile lines can be registered to one ID.

## SIM replacement

- Replacement for a damaged or lost SIM costs 30 EGP and is done in a store with the line owner's ID. The new SIM works within 30 minutes.
- Upgrading an old SIM to a 4G/5G SIM is free.

## Lost or stolen phone

- Call 1250 immediately to block the line. Blocking is free and protects the balance and bundle.
- After blocking, the customer can get a replacement SIM in a store with the same number.

## eSIM

- eSIM is available for Nile Flex and Nile Max lines on compatible phones, smartwatches and tablets.
- To activate, the customer requests an eSIM QR code in a store or, for Nile Max lines, in the My NileTel app (Profile > eSIM). The fee is 50 EGP.
- Scan the QR code from the phone settings (Mobile network > Add eSIM). The QR code can only be used once.
- Moving an eSIM to a new phone requires a new QR code (50 EGP). Deleting the eSIM from the old phone does not cancel the line.

## PIN and PUK

- The default SIM PIN is 0000. If the PIN is entered wrongly 3 times, the SIM is locked and needs the PUK code.
- The PUK code is printed on the SIM card holder and can also be obtained from 1250 after identity verification.
