# Network Coverage, 4G and 5G FAQ

## 4G coverage

- NileTel 4G covers 98% of populated areas in Egypt, including major highways.
- In areas without 4G, phones fall back to 3G automatically.

## 5G coverage

- 5G is available in selected areas of Cairo, Giza, Alexandria and the New Administrative Capital, and is expanding every quarter.
- The live coverage map is in the My NileTel app under Network > Coverage map.

## Who can use 5G

- A 5G-capable phone and a 4G/5G SIM (free upgrade in stores) are required.
- 5G is included in Max 450 and Max 750. Max 250 and Nile Flex customers can add the 5G Access add-on for 50 EGP per month.
- 5G does not use extra data; usage is counted from the same bundle.

## VoLTE and Wi-Fi Calling

- VoLTE (HD voice over 4G) and Wi-Fi Calling are free on supported phones and are enabled automatically.
- Wi-Fi Calling helps indoors where mobile signal is weak; calls are charged as normal domestic calls.

## Weak signal indoors

- Thick walls and basements reduce signal. Wi-Fi Calling is the recommended fix.
- If signal is weak across a whole area, customers should report it with their address so the network team can investigate (target 48 hours, see complaints process).

## Planned maintenance

- Customers in affected areas receive an SMS at least 24 hours before planned maintenance.
