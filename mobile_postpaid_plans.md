# Nile Max Postpaid Mobile Plans

Nile Max is NileTel's postpaid range. The customer receives a monthly bill (see billing and payments). Prices are in EGP per month and include all taxes.

## Plan prices

| Plan | Monthly fee (EGP) | Mobile data | Minutes | 5G access | Extras |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Max 250 | 250 | 20 GB | 1,000 to all networks | No (add-on available) | - |
| Max 450 | 450 | 45 GB | 2,500 to all networks | Yes | Unlimited WhatsApp |
| Max 750 | 750 | 100 GB | Unlimited to NileTel, 4,000 to other networks | Yes | One free roaming day pass per month |

## Family lines

- Any Nile Max customer can add up to 4 family lines for 120 EGP per line per month.
- Family lines share the main line's data and get 500 minutes each.

## Deposit and credit limit

- New postpaid customers pay a refundable 500 EGP deposit, unless they set up automatic payment (bank transfer or credit card).
- The credit limit is twice the monthly fee. When usage reaches the limit, outgoing services pause until a payment is made.

## Upgrades and downgrades

- Customers can upgrade to a higher plan at any time; the new fee is pro-rated on the next bill.
- Downgrades are allowed once every 6 months and take effect from the next billing cycle.
- Nile Max plans can be taken month-to-month or on a 12 or 24 month contract (see contracts and cancellation).
