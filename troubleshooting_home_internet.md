# Troubleshooting: Home Internet (DSL and Fiber Router)

## Reading the router lights

| Light | Normal | Problem and action |
|---|---|---|
| Power | Solid green | Off: check the power adapter and socket |
| DSL (DSL routers) | Solid green | Blinking: line not synchronised - check the splitter/filter and the phone cable |
| PON (Fiber routers) | Solid green | Off or blinking: fiber link down |
| LOS (Fiber routers) | Off | Red: fiber cable cut or unplugged - do not bend the cable, report an outage |
| Internet | Solid green | Off: account or authentication issue - check the bill is paid |
| Wi-Fi 2.4G / 5G | Solid or blinking green | Off: press the Wi-Fi button on the side |

## No internet

1. Restart the router: switch off, wait 30 seconds, switch on, wait 3 minutes.
2. Check that the bill is paid and the quota is not exhausted (speed drops to 1 Mbps when the quota is used up).
3. For DSL, check that every phone socket in the home has a filter and the router is connected to the splitter "Modem" port.
4. A red LOS light on a Fiber router always needs a technician - open an outage ticket.

## Slow speed

- Test speed with a cable connected to the router; Wi-Fi speeds are lower and vary by distance.
- Use the 5 GHz Wi-Fi network near the router and 2.4 GHz for longer distances.
- Many connected devices or large downloads reduce speed for everyone.

## Factory reset

- Hold the reset button for 10 seconds only as a last resort. The router then needs the customer's username and password from the welcome SMS.

## Technician visits

- Outage visits are scheduled within 24 hours (see complaints process).
- Visits are free when the fault is on the NileTel network. If the fault is caused by the customer's own equipment or wiring, the visit fee is 150 EGP, waived for Tech Assist and Device Care subscribers.
