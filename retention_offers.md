# Retention Offers Policy (Internal - Care Agents)

This internal policy tells care agents which retention offers they may give to customers who are at risk of leaving or who ask to cancel.

## Eligibility

- Retention offers are given only to customers with a **medium or high** churn risk, or to customers who explicitly ask to cancel.
- Low-risk customers receive a thank-you reward instead of a discount (RET-05).
- A customer can have at most **one price discount** (RET-01 or RET-04) active at a time, combined with at most one non-price offer (RET-02 or RET-03).
- Agents must never tell the customer their churn score; present the offer as a loyalty benefit.

## Offer catalogue

### RET-01 Contract Upgrade Discount
- For **month-to-month** customers with **high** churn risk.
- 20% off the monthly fee for 12 months when the customer moves to a One year contract.

### RET-02 Loyalty Data Boost
- For customers with tenure of **12 months or more** and medium or high risk.
- An extra 20 GB of mobile data per month (or an extra 100 GB of home quota) for 6 months.

### RET-03 Home Care Bundle
- For **DSL or Fiber** customers who do **not** have Tech Assist (tech support), with medium or high risk.
- Six months of free Tech Assist and NileTel Secure.

### RET-04 AutoPay Saver
- For customers paying by **Electronic check or Mailed check**, with medium or high risk.
- 10% off the monthly fee for 6 months when they switch to automatic bank transfer or credit card.

### RET-05 Thank-you Reward
- For **low** risk customers.
- 500 NileTel Points added to the loyalty account. No discount.

## Approval rules

- Care agents can apply discounts up to 20% directly.
- Anything above 20%, or two price discounts at once, needs team leader approval.
- Every offer must be logged in the CRM with its offer code (for example RET-01).
