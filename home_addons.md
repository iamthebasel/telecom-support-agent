# Home Internet Add-on Services

These value-added services are available to NileTel Home Internet customers (DSL or Fiber). They are billed monthly and can be cancelled at any time from the app.

## Service list

| Service | What it does | Monthly fee (EGP) |
|---|---|---|
| NileTel Secure | Network-level online security: malware and phishing blocking, parental controls | 35 |
| Cloud Backup | 100 GB of online backup for up to 5 devices | 40 |
| Device Care | Device protection: repair or replacement of the router and one registered device | 60 |
| Tech Assist | Priority technical support: remote help and technician visits within 24 hours at no visit fee | 50 |
| NileTV | Streaming TV with 80 live channels | 120 |
| NileMovies | On-demand movie streaming library | 100 |

## Home Essentials bundle

- NileTel Secure, Cloud Backup and Tech Assist together cost 100 EGP per month instead of 125 EGP.

## Notes

- Streaming services do not consume the home internet quota when watched over the NileTel network.
- Device Care claims require the device to be registered at least 30 days before the damage occurred.
