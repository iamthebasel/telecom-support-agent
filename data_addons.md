# Mobile Data Add-ons

Data add-ons can be bought on top of any Nile Flex bundle or Nile Max plan. Add-on data is consumed before the main bundle data.

## Add-on prices

| Add-on | Price (EGP) | Data | Validity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily 1 GB | 15 | 1 GB | 24 hours |
| Weekly 5 GB | 55 | 5 GB | 7 days |
| Monthly Boost 10 GB | 95 | 10 GB | Until end of the current plan cycle |
| Monthly Boost 25 GB | 190 | 25 GB | Until end of the current plan cycle |
| Social Pack | 50 | 10 GB for WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram | 30 days |
| Night Pack | 30 | 10 GB usable from 01:00 to 07:00 | 30 days |
| 5G Access | 50 per month | Enables 5G on Max 250 and Nile Flex | Monthly |

## How to buy

- Dial **\*250\*5#** or use the My NileTel app.
- Monthly Boosts can be set to auto-renew with each plan cycle; daily and weekly add-ons never auto-renew.
- Several add-ons can be active at the same time; the one expiring first is used first.

## Restrictions

- Domestic add-ons cannot be used while roaming. Roaming customers need a roaming pass (see roaming).
- Unused add-on data does not roll over.
