# NileTel Contact Channels and Store Hours

NileTel is a fictional converged telecom operator offering prepaid and postpaid mobile lines, home internet (DSL and Fiber) and IoT connectivity for businesses. All names, prices and codes in this knowledge base are fictional.

## Customer care hotline

- Dial **1250** from any NileTel line (free of charge), 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
- From other networks or from abroad, call +20 2 0000 1250 (standard international rates apply).
- Business and IoT customers have a dedicated line: **1260**, Sunday to Thursday, 9:00 to 18:00.
- Gold and Platinum loyalty members are routed to a priority queue automatically.

## My NileTel app and USSD

- The **My NileTel** app (Android and iOS) shows balance, remaining bundle, bills and open complaint tickets, and lets customers buy add-ons and pay bills.
- Dial **\*250#** to check balance and remaining bundle without the app.
- Dial **\*250\*1#** for the bundle subscription menu.

## Stores

- NileTel stores open Saturday to Thursday, 10:00 to 22:00, and Friday 14:00 to 22:00.
- Services that require identity verification (new lines, SIM replacement, contract cancellation, ownership transfer) must be done in a store with the original national ID or passport.

## Live chat and social media

- Live chat in the app and on the website is available daily from 8:00 to 24:00.
- Social media channels can answer general questions but cannot access account details; customers are asked to continue in the app chat for anything account-specific.
