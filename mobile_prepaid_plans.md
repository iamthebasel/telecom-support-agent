# Nile Flex Prepaid Mobile Bundles

Nile Flex is NileTel's prepaid range. Bundles renew every 30 days if the balance is sufficient. Prices are in Egyptian pounds (EGP) and include all taxes.

## Bundle prices

| Bundle | Price (EGP / 30 days) | Mobile data | Minutes to all networks | Extras |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Flex 75 | 75 | 4 GB | 150 | - |
| Flex 130 | 130 | 9 GB | 300 | - |
| Flex 220 | 220 | 18 GB | 600 | Unlimited WhatsApp, data rollover |
| Flex 350 | 350 | 35 GB | 1,000 | Unlimited WhatsApp, data rollover |

## How to subscribe or change bundle

- Dial **\*250\*1#** or use the My NileTel app.
- Changing to a bigger bundle takes effect immediately; the remaining days of the old bundle are not refunded.
- Changing to a smaller bundle takes effect at the next renewal date.

## Data rollover

- On Flex 220 and Flex 350, unused bundle data rolls over to the next cycle once. Rolled-over data is used first and expires at the end of the next cycle.
- Rollover is not available on Flex 75 and Flex 130.

## Out-of-bundle rates

- Calls after the bundle minutes are used: 0.50 EGP per minute.
- Data after the bundle is used: 5 EGP per 100 MB. Customers receive an SMS at 80% and 100% of bundle usage.
- To avoid out-of-bundle charges, customers can buy a data add-on (see the data add-ons document).

## Recharging

- Recharge with scratch cards, at payment kiosks, in the My NileTel app, or by bank card.
- A prepaid line becomes inactive after 90 days without any recharge, and the number is withdrawn after 180 days.
