# International Roaming

NileTel roaming lets customers use their line abroad. Countries are grouped into three zones.

## Roaming zones

- **Zone 1 (Gulf and nearby):** Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, Jordan.
- **Zone 2 (Europe):** European Union countries, United Kingdom, Switzerland, Turkey.
- **Zone 3:** All other countries.

## Activating roaming

- Postpaid (Nile Max) lines have roaming enabled by default.
- Prepaid (Nile Flex) lines must activate roaming before travel by dialling **\*250\*7#**, with a minimum balance of 100 EGP.
- Customers should enable "data roaming" in their phone settings after arrival.

## Roaming passes

| Pass | Zone | Price (EGP) | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day Pass | Zone 1 | 99 per day | 1 GB, 30 minutes |
| Day Pass | Zone 2 | 149 per day | 1 GB, 30 minutes |
| Day Pass | Zone 3 | 249 per day | 500 MB, 20 minutes |
| Week Pass | Zone 1 | 499 | 7 GB, 100 minutes |
| Week Pass | Zone 2 | 699 | 7 GB, 100 minutes |
| Hajj and Umrah Pass | Saudi Arabia | 1,250 for 30 days | 10 GB, 300 minutes |

- Day Passes start with the first usage and last 24 hours.
- Max 750 customers get one free Zone 1 or Zone 2 Day Pass per month.

## Pay-as-you-go roaming rates

- Without a pass: 10 EGP per MB of data, 30 EGP per minute for calls, 5 EGP per SMS. Receiving calls costs 15 EGP per minute.

## Bill shock protection

- Roaming data stops automatically when out-of-pass roaming charges reach 500 EGP in a billing cycle. The customer receives an SMS and can reply YES to continue.
