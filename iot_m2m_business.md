# NileTel IoT and M2M SIM Plans for Business

NileTel Business offers machine-to-machine (M2M) and IoT connectivity for companies that connect devices such as trackers, smart meters and payment terminals.

## IoT SIM plans

| Plan | Data per SIM per month | Price per SIM (EGP / month) | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| IoT Basic | 25 MB | 15 | Smart meters, sensors |
| IoT Standard | 500 MB | 45 | Fleet tracking, POS terminals |
| IoT Plus | 2 GB | 120 | Video telematics, routers |

- Data is **pooled** across all SIMs on the same business account, so heavy and light devices balance out.
- Overage beyond the pool is billed at 0.10 EGP per MB.

## Requirements and contract

- A business account needs a commercial register and tax card, and a minimum of 50 SIMs.
- Contracts are 12 or 24 months. A dedicated account manager is assigned to accounts with more than 500 SIMs.

## Network technologies

- IoT SIMs work on 2G, 4G, LTE-M and NB-IoT. NB-IoT is recommended for low-power meters in basements.
- Industrial SIMs (MFF2 embedded chip, -40 to 105 C) are available for 10 EGP extra per SIM.

## IoT Connect portal and API

- The IoT Connect portal lets customers activate, suspend and monitor SIMs and set usage alerts.
- A REST API offers the same functions for integration with the customer's own platform.
- A private APN (**iot.niletel**) with static private IPs is available for 300 EGP per month per account.

## Service level

- Network availability SLA is 99.5% per month. IoT support is on the business line 1260.
