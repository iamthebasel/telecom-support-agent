# Complaints and Escalation Process

## How to file a complaint

- Customers can file a complaint through the 1250 hotline, live chat, the My NileTel app (Support > New ticket) or in any store.
- Every complaint receives a ticket number by SMS. The customer can track the ticket in the app.

## Resolution targets

| Complaint type | Target resolution time |
|---|---|
| Home internet outage (no service) | Technician visit within 24 hours |
| Mobile network or data issue | 48 hours |
| Billing dispute | 5 working days |
| Number porting problem | 24 hours |
| Other service requests | 3 working days |

## Escalation tiers

1. **Tier 1 - Customer care:** handles the first contact and simple fixes.
2. **Tier 2 - Specialist teams:** network, billing and fixed-line technicians.
3. **Customer Advocacy team:** reviews tickets not resolved within the target time or reopened twice.

If a complaint is still unresolved after **15 working days**, the customer may escalate it to the national telecom regulator, quoting the NileTel ticket number.

## Billing disputes and refunds

- While a billing dispute is open, the disputed amount is not counted for suspension.
- Approved refunds are applied as a credit on the next bill. Cash refunds are only given when the account is closed.

## Compensation for outages

- If a home internet outage caused by NileTel lasts more than 24 hours, the customer receives a pro-rated credit for the days without service.
- Customers with repeated outages (3 or more in 60 days) are eligible for a free month of Tech Assist.
