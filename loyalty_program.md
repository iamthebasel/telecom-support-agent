# NileTel Points Loyalty Program

NileTel Points is free for all postpaid and home internet customers, and for prepaid customers who recharge at least 100 EGP per month.

## Earning points

- 10 points for every 10 EGP paid on bills or recharges.
- 200 bonus points on the customer's birthday month.
- Points expire 12 months after they are earned.

## Tiers

| Tier | Requirement | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| Silver | Less than 12 months with NileTel | Earn and redeem points |
| Gold | 12 to 36 months with NileTel | Priority hotline queue, 10% bonus points |
| Platinum | More than 36 months, or monthly bill above 750 EGP | Priority queue, 25% bonus points, one free roaming Day Pass per year |

## Redeeming points

- 1,000 points = 25 EGP bill credit or recharge.
- 400 points = 1 GB of mobile data (valid 7 days).
- 1,500 points = one month of NileMovies.
- Redeem in the My NileTel app under Rewards, or by dialling **\*250\*9#**.

## Rules

- Points cannot be transferred between customers or exchanged for cash.
- Points are cancelled if the account is closed.
